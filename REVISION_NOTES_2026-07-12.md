## B. Decisions for the committee / counsel (not changed in the file)

1. **Who is "Organizer"? (most important)** The IRVIN original makes **Kent State University the funder** paying the $10,000 stipend. The draft instead uses a generic "[ORGANIZER LEGAL ENTITY]" and demotes Kent State to an *optional academic collaborator not responsible for payment*. Decide and fix the entity: if Kent State (or an RFS entity it designates) is actually paying, name it as **Organizer**; otherwise clarify who funds and signs. This drives the signature block, §20 no-personal-liability, and the §18 indemnity direction.
2. **Governing law / forum (§27).** IRVIN used **Ohio law + Ohio Court of Claims** (appropriate for a state university). The draft offers "[New York / Ohio / Organizer's state]." Recommendation: if a public institution is a party, its counsel will often require its own state's law and forum — leave flexible but expect Ohio if Kent State is Organizer, or the institution's state may be non-negotiable for public schools.
3. **"Sublicensable" media license (§10, §11, A4/A5).** Our license to Organizer is perpetual/irrevocable/**sublicensable** (broader than the LegalZoom Copyright License's non-transferable grant). It's needed for sponsor/press use — but confirm participating **schools are comfortable** with "sublicensable," as some university IP offices push back.
4. **Public-institution indemnity (§18, last paragraph).** Many state universities **cannot contractually indemnify**. The draft already includes a fallback; expect public-school counsel to invoke it. The new attorneys'-fees clause (#4) carries a matching public-entity carve-out.
5. **Minor participants.** Participants are assumed to be adult university students. If any minors appear/participate, use a standalone **minor consent/talent-release addendum** (see `reference/legalzoom/10` and `12`) in addition to Exhibit A's guardian line.
