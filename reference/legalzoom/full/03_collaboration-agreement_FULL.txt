Collaboration Agreement

This Collaboration Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement") is made and entered on [Agreement Effective Date] (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement Date"),

By and Between [First Party Name], on behalf of [First Party_Company Name] (hereby referred to as the "First Party"); and

[Second Party Name], on behalf of [Second Party_Company Name], (hereby referred to as the "Second Party").

The First Party and the Second Party shall be collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as the "Party."

The relationship between the Parties is hereinafter referred to as the "Collaboration."

The Parties wish to mutually engage in beneficial business, bound by the terms and conditions of the Agreement.

TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT.

1. INVOLVED PARTIES.
First Party Details: [First Party Name] / [First Party Company Name] / [First Party Company Address] / [First Party Contact Number] / [First Party Email]
Second Party Details: [Second Party Name] / [Second Party Company Name] / [Second Party Company Address] / [Second Party Contact Number] / [Second Party Email]

2. PURPOSE.
The Parties wish to collaborate to establish the following Purpose (hereinafter referred to as "Purpose"). [Purpose of the Collaboration]

3. SERVICES.
"Services" means the specific tasks, deliverables, and responsibilities to be performed by each Party as set forth in this section. The Parties agree to collaborate in providing the following Services (table): Service Name / Description / Responsibilities — [Service 1..3 Name / Description / Responsibilities].

4. TERM.
The Collaboration shall come into effect on the Agreement Date and remain in effect until the Purpose of the Collaboration is served and all Services are completed.

5. AUTHORITY.
(a) Joint Decisions — (i) All significant decisions require mutual consensus or a unanimous vote. (ii) Significant decisions include the nature of Services, eligibility for Services, and funding/budget decisions.
(b) Staffing — (i) The Parties shall equally share all staffing responsibilities. (ii) Includes providing personnel and financial resources to obtain additional personnel.
(c) Representatives — (i) Each Party identifies one Representative to act on its behalf. First Party Representative: [Name]; Second Party Representative: [Name]. (ii) Each Representative keeps its Party informed of pertinent facts, developments, and milestones.

6. RESOURCE COMMITMENT, FUNDS, AND PROCEEDS.
(a) Parties share resources (funds, time, efforts, workforce, equipment, technology, infrastructure) to fulfill the Purpose.
(b) Parties equally share all financial burdens, liabilities, obligations, or costs.
(c) If outside funding/credit is required, Parties procure it jointly and share repayment.
(d) Extra capital beyond an equal share grants no additional interest/control; it is treated as a loan repaid from proceeds/profits.
(e) Remaining profits/proceeds distributed equally.

7. ADDITIONAL PARTIES.
(a) Added by unanimous decision of existing Parties. (b) Bound by this Agreement. (c) Total additional Parties not to exceed [Maximum Number]. (d) An independent consultant shall be hired to conduct a risk assessment before beginning work.

8. COLLABORATION INSURANCE.
(a) Parties maintain adequate insurance for personnel and assets against damage/loss/theft. (b) Parties name each other on their policies and indemnify/hold each other harmless except for gross or wilful misconduct or neglect.

9. TERMINATION.
(a) Terminable by mutual/unanimous agreement of Parties with significant capital shares. (b) Written notice at least [Termination Notice Period] day(s) in advance. (c) Parties immediately notified by their Representatives. (d) Assets distributed by contribution percentage unless an exception is made. (e) On dissolution, payments made to each Partner's bank account of record. (f) On termination, Parties cease reproducing/advertising/marketing/distributing Collaboration material immediately.

10. AGREEMENT EXTENSION.
(a) Extended or amended by unanimous agreement. (b) Must address date, tenure of the extension, additions of new clauses, and include the collaborating Parties' signatures.

11. DUTY OF LOYALTY.
No Party shall engage in any endeavor, venture, or business, directly or indirectly, that is competitive with or in conflict of interest with the Collaboration without the written consent of the other Parties.

12. INDEMNIFICATION.
The Parties each indemnify and hold harmless the other Parties and their permitted successors and assigns against any losses, claims, damages, penalties, liabilities, punitive damages, expenses, and reasonable legal fees resulting from the negligence of or breach of this Agreement by the indemnifying party. Survives termination.

13. ARBITRATION.
Disputes resolved by arbitration. [Number of Arbitrators] arbitrator(s) appointed by [Arbitration Appointing Party]. Venue: [Location]; Seat: [State]. Decision final and binding on all Parties.

14. ASSIGNABILITY.
Neither Party may assign the Agreement or its rights/obligations to any third party without the prior express written approval of the other Party, not to be unreasonably withheld.

15. NOTICES.
Notices delivered by certified mail, personal delivery, or overnight delivery paid by the sender.

16. FORCE MAJEURE.
Neither Party liable for failure in performance due to causes beyond reasonable control (including pandemic, fire, strike, act or order of public authority, and other acts of God) during the pendency of such event.

17. MODIFICATION.
No modification unless in writing and signed by all Parties.

18. SEVERABILITY.
If any term/clause/provision is held invalid or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, all other terms remain in full force and effect until the Agreement's termination.

19. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION.
Governed by the laws of [Governing Law]. Disputes not resolved by arbitration are resolved by litigation in the courts of [Jurisdiction], including the federal courts therein; the Parties consent to jurisdiction, accept service of process by mail, and waive jurisdictional/venue defenses.

20. LEGAL AND BINDING AGREEMENT.
This Agreement is legal and binding; each Party represents that it is eligible and authorized to enter into it.

21. ENTIRE AGREEMENT.
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding of the Parties, revokes and supersedes all prior agreements, and is intended as a final expression of their Agreement.
