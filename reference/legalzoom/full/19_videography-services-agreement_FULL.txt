This video services agreement is between ______, (the "Videographer") and ______, (the "Hiring Party").

The Hiring Party wishes to engage the Videographer as an independent contractor for the purpose of shooting videos (the "Videos") at the Hiring Party's event on ______ in ______ (the "Event"). The Videographer wishes to shoot the Videos and provide the Services at the Event. The parties therefore agree as follows:

1. ENGAGEMENT; SERVICES.
(a) Engagement. The Hiring Party retains the Videographer to provide the videography services described in subsection (b) (the "Services").
(b) Services. The Videographer shall: (i) devote as much productive time, energy, and ability as necessary to provide the Services timely; (ii) perform in a safe, good, and workmanlike manner by fully trained, skilled personnel using adequate equipment (may delegate incidental responsibilities to staff, but retains primary responsibility); (iii) provide at least ____ individuals to shoot footage, including the Videographer; (iv) shoot video at the Event for ____ hours; (v) provide all equipment and supplies; (vi) consult with the Hiring Party about specific footage/individuals.
(c) Hiring Party's Obligations: (i) make timely payments; (ii) obtain permits and permission for all locations; provide relevant information to assist the Videographer.

2. COMPENSATION.
(a) Fee. As full compensation, the Hiring Party shall pay $____ (the "Fee"): (i) $____ when signed (the "Deposit"); (ii) balance ____ days before the Event.
(b) No payment if: prohibited by law/regulation/policy; or the Videographer did not directly perform/complete the Services.
(c) The compensation is the Videographer's sole compensation.
(d) Expenses (including travel to the Event) are the Videographer's responsibility.
(e) Taxes — the Videographer is solely responsible for all income, social security, employment-related, or other taxes; the Hiring Party has no obligation to pay or withhold.
(f) Other Benefits — the Videographer has no claim for vacation pay, sick leave, retirement, social security, workers' comp, health/disability, unemployment, or any employee benefits.

3. EVENT.
(a) Schedule. The Hiring Party shall confirm the schedule of activities at least one week before the Event.
(b) Venue and Location Limitations. The Videographer is limited by the venue's rules; negotiation with officials is the Hiring Party's responsibility.
(c) Limitation of Liability. The Videographer will endeavor to produce and deliver Videos but is not responsible for requested videos not shot or missed, lack of coverage from weather, behavior of subjects, lack of light, location restrictions, other videos/photographers/flashes, or conditions beyond its control.

4. DELIVERY SCHEDULE.
The Videographer shall deliver: (a) Initial cut no later than ______; and (b) Final cut no later than ______.

5. MATERIAL EVENT CHANGES.
Any change to the time, date, or location of the Event (a "Material Event Change") after the effective date may make the Videographer unable/unavailable, impact quality, or require additional services. The Videographer shall make reasonable efforts to accommodate; if it cannot, it may terminate on written notice; if it can only by providing additional services, those and associated costs are agreed at that time.

6. ADDITIONAL SERVICES.
If the Hiring Party asks to supplement the Services and the Videographer agrees and can fulfill it, the Videographer bills additional services at then agreed-on rates.

7. VIDEOGRAPHER'S RIGHTS.
(a) Ownership Rights. The Videographer will be the sole and exclusive owner of the Videos and all rights ("Rights") in all media and all forms of exploitation whether now known or hereafter devised, including merchandising, electronic publication, multimedia, and publicity/advertising rights.
(b) Creative Rights. The Videographer may edit in its discretion, may not offer all footage, and reserves the right to release only Video deemed professional and within its artistic standards.
(c) License. The Videographer grants the Hiring Party a nonexclusive, nontransferable license to use the Videos for unlimited personal use only.

8. TERM AND TERMINATION.
(a) Term. Effective per section 20; continues until the Services are satisfactorily completed and the Videographer is paid in full.
(b) Termination: (i) by either party on ____ days' written notice, with or without cause; (ii) by either party for uncured material breach within ____ days of written notice; (iii) by the Hiring Party without notice if the Videographer fails to comply with written policies/directives or commits serious misconduct; (iv) by the Videographer immediately if it cannot accommodate a Material Event Change; (v) by the Videographer on 5 days' notice if the Hiring Party does not pay the Deposit or Fee balance.
(c) Effect of Termination: (i) the Hiring Party pays for Services rendered before termination (except where prohibited by law or the Videographer's serious misconduct); (ii) the Videographer refunds payments (excluding the Deposit) for Services not yet rendered; (iii) if the Hiring Party terminates under 8(b)(i) fewer than ____ days before the Event (except Event cancellation or Force Majeure), it forfeits payments including the Deposit and pays the remaining Fee; (iv) if terminated under 8(b)(iv), the Videographer is released from obligations; (v) no other compensation is payable after termination.

9. INDEMNIFICATION.
(a) Of Hiring Party by Videographer — indemnify against any Loss or Litigation Expense arising from (i) the Videographer's negligence or willful misconduct; or (ii) its breach of obligations/representations.
(b) Of Videographer by Hiring Party — indemnify against a Loss or Litigation Expense caused by any breach of the Hiring Party's representations or agreements.

10. FORCE MAJEURE.
No breach/default for delay/failure by reason of fire, earthquake, flood, explosion, strike, riot, war, terrorism, or similar events beyond reasonable control; affected party must (a) notify and (b) use reasonable efforts to resolve.

11. GOVERNING LAW.
(a) Choice of Law: laws of the state of ______. (b) Choice of Forum: state and federal courts in ______ County, ______.

12. AMENDMENTS.
Only effective in writing signed by both parties.

13. ASSIGNMENT AND DELEGATION.
(a) No assignment without prior written consent; (b) no delegation without the Hiring Party's prior written consent; (c) a purported assignment/delegation in violation is void.

14. COUNTERPARTS; ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES.
Counterparts each an original; electronic signatures have the same force as originals.

15. SEVERABILITY.
Invalid/illegal/unenforceable provisions do not affect the rest; construed as if never contained, unless deletion causes a material change making the transaction unreasonable.

16. NOTICES.
In writing via personal delivery, registered/certified mail (RRR), overnight courier, facsimile, or email; addresses for Videographer and Hiring Party; effective only on receipt.

17. WAIVER.
No waiver effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party; not a continuing waiver unless so specified.

18. ENTIRE AGREEMENT.
Final, complete, and exclusive; supersedes all prior and contemporaneous communications; no reliance on outside statements.

19. HEADINGS.
For convenience only; do not affect construction.

20. EFFECTIVENESS.
Effective when all parties sign; the last signature date is the agreement date.

21. NECESSARY ACTS; FURTHER ASSURANCES.
Each party uses reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to effectuate the agreement.
