This independent contractor agreement is between ______ ("the Company"), and ______ ("the Contractor"). The Company is in the business of ______ and wants to engage the Contractor to ______. The Contractor has performed the same or similar activities for others. The parties therefore agree as follows:

1. ENGAGEMENT; SERVICES.
(a) Engagement. The Company retains the Contractor to provide the services described in Exhibit A (the "Services").
(b) Services. Without limiting Exhibit A, the Contractor shall: (i) perform the Services in Exhibit A (this agreement controls on conflict); (ii) devote as much productive time, energy, and ability as necessary to provide the Services timely; (iii) perform in a safe, good, and workmanlike manner using adequate equipment in good working order; (iv) communicate progress to the Company; (v) supply all tools, equipment, and supplies (except where work is performed on Company equipment).
(c) Legal Compliance. Perform per industry standards and applicable laws; obtain all required permits/permissions.
(d) Company's Obligations. Make timely payments and notify the Contractor of procedure changes affecting obligations at least 30 days before implementing them.

2. TERM AND TERMINATION.
(a) Term. Effective per section 21; continues until the Services are satisfactorily completed and the Contractor is paid in full.
(b) Termination: (i) by either party on ____ days' written notice, with or without cause; (ii) by either party for uncured material breach within ____ days of written notice; (iii) by the Company at any time without notice if the Contractor fails to comply with written policies or reasonable directives, or commits serious misconduct.
(c) Effect. After termination, the Company shall promptly pay for Services rendered before the termination date; no other compensation is payable.

3. COMPENSATION.
(a) Per Exhibit A. (b) No payment if: prohibited by law/regulation/policy; the Contractor did not directly perform/complete the Services; Services were not to the Company's reasonable satisfaction; or Services occurred after the Term (unless agreed in writing). (c) The compensation is the Contractor's sole compensation. (d) Expenses are the Contractor's sole responsibility. (e) Taxes — the Contractor is solely responsible for all income, social security, employment-related, or other taxes; the Company has no obligation to pay or withhold. (f) Other Benefits — the Contractor has no claim for vacation pay, sick leave, retirement, social security, workers' comp, health/disability, unemployment, or any employee benefits.

4. NATURE OF RELATIONSHIP; INVENTIONS.
(a) Independent Contractor Status. (i) Independent contractors; no joint venture/partnership/agency/employer-employee relationship; neither party may create obligations for the other. (ii) The Contractor has sole right to control the means, details, manner, and method of the Services, and provides insurance for itself and its staff.
(b) Inventions Retained and Licensed. Exhibit B lists the Contractor's Prior Inventions (if none listed, none exist). If a Prior Invention is incorporated into a Company product/process/machine, the Company is granted a nonexclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide license to make, have made, modify, use, and sell it as part of that product/process/machine.
(c) Company Inventions. The Contractor has no right or interest in any work/product/documents resulting from the Services (the "Company Inventions"); these are specially commissioned "works made-for-hire," and the Company is the author and owner of all copyrights.
(d) Disclosure. The Contractor shall promptly disclose in writing all Company Inventions.
(e) Assignment. If any Company Inventions are deemed not works made-for-hire, the Contractor assigns all interest to the Company, including all copyrights, publishing rights, and rights to use/reproduce/exploit in all formats and channels now known or later created.
(f) Patent and Copyright Registrations. The Contractor shall assist the Company (at the Company's expense) to secure IP rights, sign applications/assignments, and irrevocably appoints the Company as attorney-in-fact to act on the Contractor's behalf where a signature cannot be secured.

5. USE OF TRADEMARKS.
The Contractor may use the Company's marks in connection with the Services; goodwill accrues to the Company, which remains sole owner. The Contractor may not contest, dispute, or impair the Company's interest, and has no further right to use the marks after termination unless approved in writing.

6. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION.
(a) The Contractor will treat Confidential Information as confidential and not disclose it or use it except to fulfill this agreement; must use due care to prevent unauthorized use/disclosure.
(b) Exceptions: information that becomes public (not via the Contractor's breach); was available on a nonconfidential basis before disclosure (subject to conditions); is legally compelled (with prompt notice and reasonable assistance to obtain a protective order); or was developed independently without breach.
(c) Remedy. Money damages may be insufficient; the Company may seek specific performance and injunctive or other equitable relief.

8. OTHER ACTIVITIES.
During the Term, the Contractor may engage in other independent contracting, except work inconsistent or incompatible with its obligations or the scope of Services.

9. RETURN OF PROPERTY.
Within ____ days of termination, the Contractor shall return all Company products, samples, models, property, and documents (retaining no copies). All files, records, documents, and creative work relating to the Company's business remain the Company's exclusive property.

10. INDEMNIFICATION.
(a) Of Company by Contractor. The Contractor shall indemnify the Company Indemnitees from all Claims arising from: (i) the Contractor's negligence or willful misconduct; (ii) breach of obligations/representations; (iii) breach of its representation that it is an independent contractor and compliant with applicable laws — if a court/regulator finds the Contractor is not an independent contractor based on its own actions, the Contractor assumes full responsibility for all resulting taxes, assessments, and penalties.
(b) Of Contractor by Company. The Company shall indemnify the Contractor Indemnitees from Claims arising from the Company's business operation, breach of agreements, or breach of this agreement (not where Claims result from the Contractor's own actions/inactions).

11. FORCE MAJEURE.
No breach/default for delay/failure caused by fire, earthquake, flood, explosion, strike, riot, war, terrorism, or similar events beyond reasonable control; affected party must (a) notify and (b) use reasonable efforts to resolve.

12. GOVERNING LAW.
(a) Choice of Law: laws of the state of ______. (b) Choice of Forum: state and federal courts in ______ County, ______. (c) Attorneys' Fees: the losing party reimburses the prevailing party's reasonable attorneys' fees.

13. AMENDMENTS.
Only effective in writing signed by a party.

14. ASSIGNMENT AND DELEGATION.
(a) No assignment without prior written consent; (b) no delegation without prior written consent; (c) a purported assignment/delegation in violation is void.

15. COUNTERPARTS; ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES.
Counterparts each an original; electronic signatures have the same force as originals.

16. SEVERABILITY.
Invalid/illegal/unenforceable provisions do not affect the rest; construed as if never contained, unless deletion causes a material change making the transaction unreasonable.

17. NOTICES.
In writing via personal delivery, registered/certified mail (RRR), overnight courier, or email; addresses for Company and Contractor; effective only on receipt.

18. WAIVER.
No waiver effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party; not a continuing waiver unless so specified.

19. ENTIRE AGREEMENT.
Final, complete, and exclusive; supersedes all prior and contemporaneous communications; no reliance on outside statements.

20. HEADINGS.
For convenience only; do not affect construction.

21. EFFECTIVENESS.
Effective when all parties sign; the last signature date is the agreement date.

22. NECESSARY ACTS; FURTHER ASSURANCES.
Each party uses reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to effectuate the agreement.
