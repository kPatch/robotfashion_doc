Volunteer Agreement

This Volunteer Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement") is entered into and shall be effective as on [Effective Date] (hereinafter referred to as the "Effective Date"),

By and Between
[Volunteer Name] (hereinafter referred to as the "Volunteer"), having their primary address at [Volunteer's Address] and;
[Organization Name] (hereinafter referred to as the "Organization"), having its principal place of business at [Organization Address].

The Volunteer and the Organization are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as the "Party."

WHEREAS the Volunteer is willing to provide its time and services to the Organization;
AND WHEREAS the Organization would like to accept such services by the Volunteer;
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants herein, the Parties do hereby agree as follows:

TERMS AND CONDITIONS.

1. VOLUNTEER SERVICES.
The Volunteer will be working with the Organization in the capacity of [Volunteer Role]. As a [Volunteer Role], the Volunteer will be providing the following Services ("Volunteering Services" or "Services"): [Specify the Volunteering Services]. The Volunteer will provide their Services to the Organization for [Volunteering Hours per Week] hour(s) every week.

2. WAGES.
The Volunteer understands that this is a role without any salary, compensation, or other benefits. This is an exclusive voluntary position to assist the Organization.

3. RELATIONSHIP OF PARTIES.
Nothing contained herein shall be taken to establish any partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship. Volunteer at all times will be a volunteer only and shall receive no benefits from the Organization.

4. SUPERVISOR.
The Volunteer shall be directed by the Supervisor [Supervisor's Name]. The Volunteer agrees to follow all directions given by the Supervisor, along with all rules and instructions provided by the Organization.

5. INDEMNITY.
(a) The Organization hereby agrees to indemnify the Volunteer against any damages and losses as well as legal fees or any cost incurred in the act or omission of the Organization;
(b) The Volunteer hereby agrees to indemnify the Organization against any damages related to the Volunteer's activity.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY.
The Volunteer agrees that any information obtained during work shall remain confidential to the Organization. The Volunteer shall not disclose any confidential information to any third party.

7. TERM.
The duration of this Agreement is [Agreement Term in Months] month(s) commencing from the Effective Date. Upon expiration of this Agreement, the agreement between the Organization and Volunteer shall come to an end, and neither Party intends for a contractual employment relationship.

8. VOLUNTEER'S EXPENSES.
The Volunteer may be reimbursed for the following expenses: [Expenses Eligible for Reimbursement]

9. VOLUNTEER'S RESPONSIBILITY.
The Volunteer agrees to abide by the guidelines and act in good faith on behalf of the Organization. The Volunteer further agrees to maintain confidentiality and carry out the tasks outlined in the role description.

10. ORGANIZATION'S RESPONSIBILITY.
The Organization agrees to provide the Volunteer with information, supervision, support, training, and all other necessary information to conduct the Organization's activities.

11. TERMINATION.
For any unforeseen circumstance whatsoever and upon the mutual consent of both Parties, the Parties may terminate this Agreement at any time they deem important. Upon termination of this Agreement, the Parties shall not be bound by the terms set out herein, and hence, the Volunteer shall not be obligated to the Organization's interest in any form, except for Confidentiality clauses that involve indefinite commitment.

12. ARBITRATION.
In the event of any dispute arising in and out of this Agreement between the Parties, it shall be resolved by arbitration. There shall be [Number of Arbitrators] arbitrator(s), who shall be appointed by [Arbitration Appointing Party Name]. The venue of arbitration shall be [Location of Arbitration], and the Seat shall be [State of Seat]. The arbitrators' decision shall be final and binding on both Parties.

13. SEVERABILITY.
If any provision of this Agreement is found unenforceable under applicable law, then the Parties agree that such provision shall be excluded from this Agreement, and the remaining provisions shall be enforceable in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

14. GOVERNING LAW.
This Agreement herein shall be governed and interpreted in accordance with the laws of [Governing Law].

15. ENTIRE AGREEMENT.
This is the entire agreement regarding the terms and conditions of the Company's engagement. It supersedes all agreements previously entered into by the Parties.
