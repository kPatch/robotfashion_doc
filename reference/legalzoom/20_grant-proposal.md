# LegalZoom — Grant Proposal (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/grant-proposal
**Not a legal agreement** — a funding-request/pitch document.

Structure: Introduction (ask $X for [Project]) · About Us · Mission & Vision · Our Team · Success Stories · Problem · Methods & Strategies · **Plan of Evaluation** (track KPIs, review at intervals, adjust) · **Funds Required** (cost-breakdown table: Labor / Equipment / Suppliers / Other / Total incl. tax) · Timeline table · minimal T&Cs (privacy, cancellation, third-party, warranties/severability) · signatures.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Wrong direction for us (this is the *applicant asking a grantor*; RFS is the *funder*). No clauses to import. Only useful as a **reference for language** if RFS itself seeks outside grant funding, or to shape the **Exhibit B reporting** expectation (cost-breakdown + KPI/evaluation format). Our draft §6 (records/expense summary) and Exhibit B (reporting row) already cover the reporting side lightly; the cost-element table here is a good model for the expense summary the Institution provides.
