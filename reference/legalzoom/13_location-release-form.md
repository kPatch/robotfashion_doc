# LegalZoom — Location Release Form (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/location-release-form
Producer ↔ Owner. Standalone form for filming/photographing at a third-party property.

Key clauses: 1 Permission & Responsibilities — Owner grants entry/use for Filming and won't bring privacy/publicity/defamation claims; Producer must (i) leave location as found (normal wear/force majeure excepted), (ii) use reasonable care, (iii) comply with Owner's security/safety, (iv) comply with laws/codes/ordinances, (v) obtain third-party releases/permits. 2 Location Use (equipment/props/personnel; restore furniture). 3 Compensation/Location Fee + reimbursement/invoice. 4 Parties' Rights — Producer owns Footage, may use in all media in perpetuity worldwide, may reschedule for weather; Owner may remove Producer for safety non-compliance. 5 Owner reps sole ownership/authority, no third-party permission needed. 6 **Indemnification + Insurance** — Producer indemnifies Owner for its negligence/willful misconduct & breach; Owner indemnifies Producer for its breach (not for Producer's own acts); Producer carries commercial liability insurance naming Owner as additional insured. 7–19 standard boilerplate.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Not part of the institution agreement. Use as a **standalone form only if** RFS films/rehearses at a school, studio, or private venue not already covered by the Event venue contract. Practical borrows for such shoots: "leave location as found," reasonable-care, and additional-insured requirements. No changes needed to the main agreement.
