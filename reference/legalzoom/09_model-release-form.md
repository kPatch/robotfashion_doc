# LegalZoom — Model Release Form (Adults) (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/model-release-form
Model → Photographer, for stated payment.

1. **Permission to take & use** — full irrevocable right to take Photographs and use/publish/reproduce/edit/exhibit/license/distribute/exploit, whole or part. No obligation to use.
2. **Assignment; Waiver** — Model assigns all interest incl. copyright; photos are Photographer's sole property; Model waives right to (a) inspect/approve photos; (b) inspect/approve accompanying text/graphics/content; (c) receive royalties/compensation.
3. **Release** — releases from liability from Photographic Use, "including that based on **copyright infringement, invasion of privacy, right of publicity, libel, defamation, or false light, or resulting from any alteration, blurring, optical illusion, use in composite form, distortion, or other modification**" intentional or otherwise.
4. **Model's Representations** — (a) legal adult, full capacity; (b) no conflicting commitment; (c) has read and fully understands.
- Signature block collects SSN. **⚠ Do NOT replicate SSN collection** in our forms (unnecessary and sensitive).

**Verdict vs our draft (Exhibit A §A4):** Strong borrow list:
- Add **"false light"** and the **"alteration, blurring, use in composite form, distortion, or other modification, intentional or otherwise"** language — this defeats a "the edit made me look bad" claim. Important for a livestreamed/edited robot show.
- Add explicit **waiver of the right to inspect or approve** the finished media and any accompanying text/graphics.
- Keep our gross-negligence carve-out.
- Do not collect SSNs.
