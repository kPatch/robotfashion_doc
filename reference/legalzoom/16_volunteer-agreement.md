# LegalZoom — Volunteer Agreement (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/volunteer-agreement
Organization ↔ Volunteer. Standalone form for directly-engaged unpaid volunteers.

Key clauses: 1 Volunteer Services (role, services, hours/week) · 2 **Wages — none** (no salary/compensation/benefits) · 3 **Relationship — not employment/partnership/JV** · 4 Supervisor (volunteer follows directions/rules) · 5 Mutual Indemnity · 6 Confidentiality · 7 Term · 8 Expense reimbursement (specified) · 9 Volunteer responsibility (good faith, confidentiality) · 10 Organization responsibility (info, supervision, support, training) · 11 Termination (mutual; confidentiality survives) · 12–15 Arbitration/Severability/Governing Law/Entire Agreement.

Guidance flags **worker-misclassification risk**: "avoid sounding like an employment contract," state no-wage/no-benefits clearly.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Not part of the institution agreement. Use as a **standalone form if RFS itself recruits unpaid volunteers** (fittings, production, event ops). If instead the Institution's own student team assists, they're already **"Participants"** under our draft (§2) and sign Exhibit A — no separate volunteer agreement needed. Key borrow only if RFS uses its own volunteers: explicit **no-wages / not-employment** clause to avoid misclassification.
