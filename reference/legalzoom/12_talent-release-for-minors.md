# LegalZoom — Talent Release Form for Minors (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/talent-release-form-for-minors

Same structure as the adult **Talent Release** (permission to record likeness/voice/performance in all media; assignment/waiver of inspect-approve + royalties; release from distortion/adverse-result liability), except the **parent/legal guardian** signs on behalf of the minor Talent and warrants (a) legal adult with capacity and (b) parent/guardian with full authority to consent. Recommend both parents sign; sign in person; jurisdiction's age of majority governs.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Contingency only (participants expected to be adult university students). Draft §9 + Exhibit A parent/guardian line already cover it. Keep this as a standby minor-appearance addendum. Don't collect SSNs.
