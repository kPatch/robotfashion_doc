# LegalZoom — Copyright License Agreement for Artwork (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/copyright-license-agreement-for-artwork
**Directly relevant** — models licensing a creative work **while the creator keeps ownership** (= our student-designs structure; student = Licensor, Organizer = Licensee).

Key clauses:
1. **Grant** — nonexclusive, nontransferable license to exercise specified rights in all media (now known or later developed).
2. **Restrictions** — limited to stated uses; no sublicensing; no modification/derivative works beyond grant without consent; grant covers only Artwork controlled by Licensor.
3. **No Assignment or Transfer** — "license rights only ... **the Licensor retains ownership of the copyright in the Artwork, and all rights not expressly granted**."
4. **Credit** — mandatory credit line; if the credit/acknowledgment isn't shown as specified, **all permissions are cancelled**.
8. **Ownership & Use** — Licensee acknowledges Licensor's ownership, may not claim ownership, **may not challenge Licensor's title or the validity** of the copyright/agreement.
9. **Licensor Representations** — (a) sole owner; (b) right to grant; (c) **original, not in the public domain, not obscene**; (d) not previously transferred/exclusively licensed/encumbered; (e) not aware of infringement/third-party claims; (f) no third-party consents needed; (g) not created within scope of third-party employment.
11 Indemnification · 12 Termination (auto-terminate on misuse, unauthorized transfer, missing credit, non-publication within a year) · 13–23 boilerplate.

**Verdict vs our draft (§10 Ownership; §A5 Ownership & License):** Strong validation + borrows:
- Our §10 already: students retain ownership; Organizer gets a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual license**. Note ours is broader (perpetual/irrevocable/sublicensable) than this template's nontransferable license — reasonable for promotional/archival use; keep it but **confirm the school is comfortable with "sublicensable"** (needed for sponsor/press use).
- Add to §10/§A6 the crisp reps: work is **"original, not in the public domain, and does not contain anything obscene,"** and **not created within the scope of a third party's employment**.
- Add the "**retains ownership of ... all rights not expressly granted**" catch-all to §10 — makes the ownership reservation airtight.
- Keep our **credit/attribution** language (§10 para 3); this template's "no credit → license cancelled" is stricter than we need, but confirms crediting matters.
