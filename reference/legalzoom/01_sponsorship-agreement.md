# LegalZoom — Sponsorship Agreement (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/sponsorship-agreement
Attorney-drafted. Extracted from the free on-page template (full download requires paid LegalZoom account).

## Template body

This sponsorship agreement is between ___ (the "Organizer") and ___ (the "Sponsor").

The Organizer is engaged in the organization and production of ___, known as ___, on ___ and located at ___ (the "Event").

The Sponsor wishes to sponsor the Event by providing ___ (the "Sponsorship") in exchange for certain rights to be granted in connection with the Event.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

**1. GRANT OF RIGHTS.** In exchange for the Sponsorship Fee (section 2), the Sponsor will receive the following rights (the "Sponsorship Rights"):
- (a) Promotional Rights — right to sell or give away Sponsor's products/services at the Event; Organizer provides appropriate space; Sponsor retains proceeds.
- (b) Event Rights — [fillable].
- (c) Media Rights — (i) named in all press releases; (ii) subject to prior approval, credits/mentions in media promotion; use footage of Event for Sponsor's promotional purposes.

**2. SPONSORSHIP FEE.** [fillable financial terms]

**4. TERM; TERMINATION.**
- (a) Term — effective as of Effective Date, continues until ___ ("Term") unless terminated.
- (b) Termination — (i) either party on ___ days' written notice, with/without cause; (ii) either party for uncured material breach within ___ days of written notice; (iii) either party on written notice if the other's representations prove materially inaccurate; (iv) either party without notice if the other is convicted of a crime/offense, fails to comply with written policies or reasonable directives, or commits serious misconduct; Sponsor may terminate with/without cause but forfeits payments already made and pays remaining balance within 10 days ("Termination Fee").

**6. EXCLUSIVITY OF SPONSORSHIP.** Organizer will not permit any competitor listed on Exhibit A to sponsor/supply/associate with the Event. Organizer uses reasonable efforts to prevent/prosecute non-sponsor competitors weakening the Sponsorship.

**7. NO COMPETING SPONSORSHIPS.** From 30 days before to 30 days after the Event, Sponsor may not sponsor a competing event within 10 miles without Organizer's written consent.

**8. ORGANIZER RESPONSIBILITIES.** Organizer shall: (a) organize/produce/supervise in a workmanlike manner, per law, with skilled personnel; (b) arrange venue use, permits, parking/transport, equipment, vendors; (c) provide professional security and reasonable safety steps; (d) use best efforts for media coverage; (e) use best efforts to promote and maximize attendance.

**9. TRADEMARKS.**
- (a) Sponsor Trademarks — (i) nonexclusive limited license to Organizer for Event promotion only, written consent before each use (deemed rejected if no response within 10 days); (ii) ownership retained by Sponsor; (iii) no-infringement warranty; (iv) delivery within 10 days of Effective Date.
- (b) Organizer Trademarks — mirror provisions; delivery within 45 days.

**11. INDEMNIFICATION.**
- (a) Of Sponsor by Organizer — indemnify against any Loss / Litigation Expense arising from (i) inaccuracy of Organizer's representation; (ii) Organizer's breach; (iii) the Event (bodily injury, death, property loss) but only to the extent of Organizer's negligent/intentional acts. Organizer maintains sufficient liability insurance.
- (b) Of Organizer by Sponsor — mirror for Sponsor's representations/breach.

**12. INSURANCE.** Policies maintained during Term + 48 months after. (a) Each party maintains commercial general liability (limits per Exhibit B), endorsement naming the other as additional insured; (b) Organizer obtains workers' comp; (c) Organizer obtains event cancellation insurance. Certificates provided at least 10 days before Event.

**13. RIGHT TO POSTPONE EVENT.** Sponsor may request postponement if a legitimate threat of injury/harm exists to Sponsor, personnel, property, or the public.

**14. FORCE MAJEURE.** (a) No breach for delay/failure due to fire, earthquake, flood, explosion, strike, riot, war, terrorism, or similar events beyond reasonable control; affected party notifies and uses reasonable efforts to resolve. (b) Event Cancellation — if Event cancelled by force majeure, Sponsor owes no future payments and Organizer refunds payments received (proportionate refund for partial cancellation).

**15. GOVERNING LAW; ATTORNEYS' FEES.** (a) choice of law ___; (b) choice of forum ___; (c) prevailing party recovers reasonable attorneys' fees.

**16. AMENDMENTS.** Only effective in writing signed by a party/authorized rep.

**17. ASSIGNMENT AND DELEGATION.** (a) no assignment without prior written consent; (b) no delegation without consent; (c) violating assignment/delegation is void.

**18. COUNTERPARTS; ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES.** Counterparts allowed; electronic signatures have same force as originals.

**19. SEVERABILITY.** Invalid provisions severed; rest construed as if never included, unless deletion causes a material change making the transaction unreasonable.

**20. NOTICES.** (a) writing + permitted delivery methods (personal, mail RRR, overnight courier, fax, email); (b) addresses; (c) effective only on receipt.

**21. WAIVER.** No waiver unless in writing signed by waiving party; not a continuing waiver unless specified.

**22. ENTIRE AGREEMENT.** Final, complete, exclusive; prior/contemporaneous communications merged and superseded; no reliance on outside statements.

**23. HEADINGS.** For convenience only; do not affect construction.

**24. EFFECTIVENESS.** Effective when all parties sign; date = last signature date.

**25. NECESSARY ACTS; FURTHER ASSURANCES.** Each party takes reasonable actions to effectuate the agreement.

Signature blocks (Date / By / Name for each party). Exhibit A — List of Sponsor's Competitors. Exhibit B — Insurance Coverage table (Responsible Party / Type / Description / Limits).

## Notable how-to guidance
- Organizer templates typically limit organizer obligations/liabilities: sponsor's promise not to sponsor competing events nearby for a period; limits on organizer responsibility for property damage/injury/death; limits on organizer obligations if the event is cancelled for reasons beyond control.
- Recommended provisions: renewals, exclusivity/competitive restrictions, trademark use rules, confidentiality of terms, security, merchandise, indemnity, rebates, insurance.
- Check state non-compete law if restricting the sponsor's ability to host/sponsor other events.
