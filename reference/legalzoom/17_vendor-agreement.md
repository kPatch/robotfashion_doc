# LegalZoom — Vendor Agreement (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/vendor-agreement
Company ↔ Vendor. Master-agreement + **Statement of Work (SOW)** structure.

Key clauses: 1 Services (per SOWs; Agreement prevails over SOW on conflict) · 2 Fees (monthly invoices, net-N days, tax-inclusive) · 3 Reps/warranties (best efforts, workmanlike, lawful, non-infringing) · 4 **IP — work-made-for-hire to Company** · 5 Confidentiality (+ **equitable/injunctive relief** for breach) · 6 Indemnification (Vendor→Company for negligence/breach/IP infringement) · 7 **Limitation of Liability** (no consequential damages; cap = fees paid, except indemnity) · 8 Term & Termination (Company may terminate **for no cause** on notice) · 9–16 Governing Law/Assignment/Notices/Independent-contractor relationship/Force Majeure/Amendments/Entire Agreement/**Survival**.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Not part of the institution agreement. Use if RFS separately engages **production vendors, fabricators, or robotics support**. Two borrow-worthy structural ideas for our main agreement:
- **Explicit Survival clause** — our §17 lists surviving sections inline; a dedicated "Survival" catch-all ("provisions that by their nature should survive...") is already partly present — fine, could be tightened.
- **Equitable/injunctive relief for breach of Confidentiality** — add to our §15 (Confidentiality) that breach may cause irreparable harm entitling Organizer to injunctive relief. Good, cheap addition.
- Note our Exhibit B already behaves like an SOW; keep that.
