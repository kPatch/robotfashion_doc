# LegalZoom — Talent Release Form (Adults) (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/talent-release-form
Talent → Creator. Like the Model Release but explicitly covers **performance** (live/recorded).

1. **Permission to make & use Recordings** — Creator may record Talent's **likeness, voice, and performance in all media** and use/edit/exhibit/license/distribute/exploit. Guidance: also covers use of Talent's **name and biographical information** and credits; can restrict to a named project or prohibited uses. No obligation to use.
2. **Assignment; Waiver** — assigns all interest incl. copyright; waives inspect/approve + royalties; if Talent gets a copy, may not authorize others' use.
3. **Release** — releases Creator from liability for "any distortion or illusionary effect or other adverse result" from publication, and violation of any personal/proprietary right in the Recordings.
4. **Reps** — legal adult, full capacity; no conflicting commitment. (Signature block again asks for SSN — skip.)

**Verdict vs our draft:** This is the closest analog to what our participants (student designers, models on the runway, interviewees) actually do — a **live performance/appearance release**. Our Exhibit A §A4 already grants image/likeness/voice/**performance**/interview rights — good. Borrow:
- Confirm inclusion of **"performance"** and **"biographical information"** in A4 (A4 already lists biography/performance — good).
- Allow the participant to note **prohibited uses / restrictions** (optional line) — reasonable goodwill gesture for schools.
- Keep gross-negligence carve-out; don't collect SSNs.
