# LegalZoom — Collaboration Agreement (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/collaboration-agreement

Structure assumes a **peer joint venture**: equal sharing of resources, costs, liabilities, and profits; joint/unanimous decisions; shared staffing; extra capital treated as a loan, not extra control; assets distributed by contribution % on termination.

Sections: 1 Involved Parties · 2 Purpose · 3 Services (name/description/responsibilities table) · 4 Term (until Purpose served) · 5 Authority (joint decisions, staffing, **Representatives** — each party names one rep to keep the others informed) · 6 Resource Commitment/Funds/Proceeds · 7 Additional Parties · 8 Collaboration Insurance (name each other on policies; mutual indemnity save gross/willful misconduct) · 9 Termination · 10 Extension · 11 Duty of Loyalty (no competing/conflicting ventures without consent) · 12 Indemnification (survives termination) · 13 Arbitration · 14 Assignability (consent not unreasonably withheld) · 15 Notices · 16 Force Majeure · 17 Modification · 18 Severability · 19 Governing Law & Jurisdiction · 20 Legal & Binding · 21 Entire Agreement.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Structurally a poor fit — our relationship is asymmetric (Organizer funds; Institution participates), not an equal-share collaboration. Do NOT import the shared-cost/shared-profit or joint-decision mechanics. Borrow-worthy only:
- The **"Representative"** concept (each Party names one authorized point-of-contact) — our draft already names contacts; could formalize.
- The optional **Academic Collaborator** framing (Kent State) resembles this template's spirit and should stay in a separate signed instrument, not this bilateral agreement.
- Confirms adding a **severability** clause is standard.
