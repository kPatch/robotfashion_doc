# LegalZoom — Fashion Show Sponsorship Quote (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/fashion-show-sponsorship-quote
**Not a legal agreement** — a pricing quote. Structure: Quote details (number, dates, preparer, expiration) · Organizer details · Client details · **Sponsorship Packages** table (Package / Subtotal / Quantity / Total, then Subtotal / Tax / Discount% / Grand Total) · brief T&Cs (the Event; the Sponsorship; **Sponsorship Credit** given in exchange; Relationship = this document only; Cancellation on N days' notice; Governing Law) · signatures. Marked proprietary/confidential.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Not relevant to the institution agreement. Useful only as a **fashion-show-specific sponsor pricing/package sheet** if RFS quotes corporate sponsors. Confirms the funding-flow direction is *inbound sponsor → RFS*, whereas our agreement is *RFS → institution*. No clauses to import.
