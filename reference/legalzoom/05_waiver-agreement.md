# LegalZoom — Waiver Agreement (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/waiver-agreement

One-directional liability release (Client releases Company). Sections:
1. **Waiver** — "In consideration of participating in any way with the Company, its related events, and activities, the Client hereby **releases, waives, discharges, and covenants not to sue** the Company, its employees, trustees, or agents from any and all claims."
2. **Assumption of Risk** — participation is voluntary; Client assumes any risk; release "with no bounds of limitation," including attorney's fees, personal injury, property damage, and coherent losses/damages.
3. **Indemnification** — Client indemnifies/holds harmless the Company; survives termination.
4. **Right to Cancel** — Company may refuse association if it foresees damage.
5. Acknowledgment · 6 Arbitration · 7 Governing Law · 8 Entire Agreement · 9 Severability.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Our Exhibit A (A2 Assumption of Risk, A3 Release of Claims) already tracks this and is more thorough (retains the gross-negligence/willful-misconduct carve-out, which this template lacks). Borrow-worthy:
- Add the explicit verb string **"release, waive, discharge, and covenant not to sue"** to Exhibit A §A3 (currently just "releases ... from any claims"). "Covenant not to sue" is a meaningful add.
- Consider naming **"trustees"** among released parties for institutional participants.
- Keep our gross-negligence/willful-misconduct exception (better than this template's unbounded release, which courts often trim anyway).
