# LegalZoom — Submission Release Form (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/submission-release-form
Letter-style release: creator submits "Work" (described in Annex A) for review.

Key clauses:
1. Release is a **prerequisite to review**; governs the Work whether submitted before/after/contemporaneously; **"No confidential or fiduciary relationship"** is established by submitting.
2. **Originality rep** — sole creator/owner, full authority, no prior grants, review won't violate third-party rights (copyright, privacy, publicity).
3. **Independent creation** — reviewer may have independently created/received/accessed similar or identical ideas; creator not entitled to compensation for use of such similar/identical ideas; reviewer not liable for using any part that is "neither novel nor legally protected"; reviewer may **not** use a legally protected portion without a signed compensation agreement.
4. **No obligation** — no contract beyond this release; review doesn't obligate use or negotiation; creator retains rights to submit elsewhere.
5. **Indemnity** — creator indemnifies against claims from breach of reps (incl. reasonable outside attorneys' fees).
6. No return of copy; release from liability for loss/damage to the Work.
7–12. Governing law · entire agreement · severability · amendment · "you" includes parents/affiliates/employees/agents/successors/assigns · **executed voluntarily, fully understanding contents**.
Annex A: description of work + copyright info.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Genuinely useful additions the draft lacks:
- **Independent-creation / no-confidential-relationship** protection — shields Organizer if a similar robot-garment concept appears from another school or partner. Worth adding to §10 or Exhibit A as a "Submissions" protection.
- The clean **originality representation** (sole creator/owner, no third-party violation) — our §10/§A6 already have this; fine.
- "**Executed voluntarily, fully understanding contents**" acknowledgment — add to Exhibit A signature block.
- Note: this template's independent-creation stance is about *ideas/novelty*; keep it consistent with our promise that students **retain ownership of their protected designs**.
