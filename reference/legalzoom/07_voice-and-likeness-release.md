# LegalZoom — Release & Authorization to Use Voice and Likeness (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/release-and-authorization-to-use-voice-and-likeness
**HIGH VALUE for Exhibit A media authorization.**

Structure: Releasor (individual) → Recorder (company). Sections:
1. **Payment** — consideration; no additional consideration due.
2. **Participation** — Releasor participates in photos/video/audio "Recordings"; Recorder has permission to record voice, sounds, conversation, image, likeness.
3. **Grants** — perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free, fully paid-up license to unlimited use of likeness/voice in any media now known or later created, for development, marketing, advertising, licensing, sale, distribution, promotion of any events/products/merchandise/services/brands. "Likeness" defined broadly (likeness, voice, copyrights, persona, character, image, logos, slogans, catch phrases, art, physical attributes incl. body art/tattoos). Recordings treated as **works-made-for-hire / exclusive property of Recorder**; Releasor **assigns** all interest. Recorder may edit/alter/distort/modify/broadcast/duplicate/distribute worldwide. **Releasor has no right to approve any use.** No third-party approval or payment.
4. **Release** — releases Recorder from all claims now or later arising from the release or use of the Likeness/Recordings, **including claims for personal injury, invasion of privacy, defamation, libel, right of publicity, infliction of emotional distress, or additional payment.**
5. **No Obligation to Use.**
6. **Releasor's Representations** — (a) at least 18 / not a minor; (b) full right and legal capacity; (c) no conflicting commitment or legal impediment; (d) identifying info correct; (e) entered voluntarily without reliance on outside promises; (f) has read and understands.
7. **Assignment** — personal to Releasor, not assignable by Releasor; Recorder may assign/sublicense/sell/pass-through freely without approval.
8–15. Governing Law · Amendments · Counterparts/e-sign · Severability · Notices · Waiver · Entire Agreement · Headings.

**Verdict vs our draft (Exhibit A §A4 Media & Likeness, §A5 Ownership):** Our A4 is solid but should borrow:
- **Enumerate the released claim types**: add "including any claim for invasion of privacy, right of publicity, defamation, libel, or infliction of emotional distress" to A3/A4.
- Add **"no right to approve or inspect"** the finished use, and **no additional compensation / no third-party approval** language explicitly.
- Add representation that the participant **has read and understood** and **enters voluntarily** (strengthens enforceability).
- **DO NOT import the work-made-for-hire / full assignment of the Recordings** wholesale. Our project intentionally lets students **retain ownership of their designs** (per IRVIN original §M and draft §10/§A5). Distinguish: Organizer may own/edit the *photos & video it captures*, but students keep ownership of the *garments/designs*, licensing them to Organizer. Keep that separation crisp.
