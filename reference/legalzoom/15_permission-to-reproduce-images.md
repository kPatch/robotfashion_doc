# LegalZoom — Permission to Reproduce Images (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/permission-to-reproduce-images
Narrow letter-agreement: copyright **Owner** grants a **Requestor** permission to reproduce specific pre-existing Image(s).

Key terms: identifies Image(s) (author/title/date/medium/dimension); one-time Fee (permission ineffective until paid); **nonexclusive**, single-use, single-language rights; mandatory **credit line** (missing credit → permission cancelled); Owner **makes no representations / has obtained no releases** and doesn't warrant non-infringement; **Requestor indemnifies Owner**; auto-terminates if not published within N years; no IP transfer, non-severable, non-transferable.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Not part of the institution agreement. Use **only if** RFS reproduces third-party or school-supplied images (existing design documentation, archival photos) where a school/third party owns the copyright. Practical takeaway: if a school provides pre-existing images, get this permission separately and note that the school warrants nothing about third-party rights — RFS bears that risk. No change to main agreement; our §10 originality reps already push that risk onto the Institution for Work Product it submits.
