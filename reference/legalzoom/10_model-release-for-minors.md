# LegalZoom — Model Release Form for Minors (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/model-release-form-for-minors

**Identical to the adult Model Release**, except: the **Guardian** signs "on behalf of" the Model (minor). Representation §4 changes to: Guardian (a) is a legal adult with capacity; (b) **is the parent or legal guardian with full authority to consent on behalf of the Model**; (c) no conflicting commitment; has read and understands. Two signature lines for guardians.

Practical guidance worth following:
- Get **written consent of BOTH parents** where possible, to avoid inter-parent disputes.
- Sign **in person** with minor + guardian(s) + photographer present.
- Age of majority is **state-specific** (usually 18).

**Verdict vs our draft:** Our participants are expected to be university students (adults), so minors forms are a **contingency**. Our draft already handles this correctly: §9 (Participant Releases) requires guardian consent for anyone under the age of majority, and Exhibit A has a parent/guardian signature line. Recommendation: keep a **separate minor-consent addendum** on hand (this template) rather than complicating the main Exhibit A; reference "age of majority in the applicable jurisdiction" (draft already does). Don't collect SSNs.
