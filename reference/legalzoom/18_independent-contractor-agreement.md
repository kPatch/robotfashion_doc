# LegalZoom — Independent Contractor Agreement (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/independent-contractor-agreement
Company ↔ Contractor. Robust services contract (Exhibit A duties/comp; Exhibit B prior inventions).

Key clauses: 1 Engagement/Services (workmanlike; supplies own tools) · 2 Term/Termination · 3 Compensation (sole comp; **contractor pays all taxes; no employee benefits**) · 4 **Nature of Relationship; Inventions** — strong independent-contractor status; **Company Inventions = work-made-for-hire + assignment + power-of-attorney**; contractor's Prior Inventions stay contractor's but Company gets nonexclusive license if incorporated · 5 Use of Trademarks (goodwill accrues to Company) · 6 **Confidentiality** (detailed exceptions + **injunctive relief**) · 8 Other Activities (no incompatible work) · 9 Return of Property · 10 **Indemnification** incl. **misclassification indemnity** (contractor covers taxes/penalties if reclassified as employee) · 11 Force Majeure · 12 Governing Law + **attorneys' fees (prevailing party)** · 13–22 boilerplate.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Not part of the institution agreement. Use if RFS hires **individual producers, coordinators, photographers, or technical contractors** directly. Borrows:
- Confirms our §28 (Relationship of Parties) is adequate but short; if RFS engages individuals, use this full IC form with the **misclassification indemnity + own-taxes** language.
- **Injunctive relief for confidentiality breach** — reinforces the same borrow noted from the Vendor template for our §15.
- **Attorneys'-fees / prevailing-party** clause — again flagged as a gap in our main agreement (add to §27).
- ⚠ Its **work-made-for-hire / assignment** IP model is the OPPOSITE of our student-ownership model — do NOT import into the Institution/Participant context; students keep ownership and license to Organizer.
