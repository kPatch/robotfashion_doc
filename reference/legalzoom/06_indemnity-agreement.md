# LegalZoom — Indemnity Agreement (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/indemnity-agreement

One-way indemnity (Indemnifying Party protects Indemnified Party). Sections:
1. **General** — (a) indemnify and hold harmless "from and against any and all claims, liabilities, loss, expenses, suits, damages, judgments, demands, and costs (including reasonable attorney and other legal fees and expenses)" arising out of the Indemnifying Party's/third party's acts, "including but not limited to any loss or damage of property or ... accident, injury, or death of persons," **except to the extent damages are solely directed to the negligence of the Indemnified Parties**; (b) defense/settlement borne by Indemnifying Party at its sole expense regardless of success; (c) obligation lasts perpetually until terminated.
2. **Period of Limitation** — no claim after [period]; claims extinguished unless timely filed.
3. Arbitration · 4 **Subrogation** (indemnifying party subrogated to indemnified party's recovery rights on payment) · 5 Termination · 6 Non-waiver · 7 Amendments · 8 Counterparts · 9 Notices · 10 Entire Agreement · 11 Governing Law & Jurisdiction.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Our §18 indemnity is already broader and better-structured (enumerated trigger list (a)–(h), defense-control + no-settlement-without-consent, public-institution fallback in §18 last paragraph). Borrow-worthy items:
- The compact damages enumeration "**claims, liabilities, loss, expenses, suits, damages, judgments, demands, and costs (including reasonable attorney and other legal fees)**" — our draft is close; fine as-is.
- Optional: a **defense "at its sole expense regardless of outcome"** phrase.
- The **public-institution carve-out already in our §18** is the right instinct — Kent State (state university) generally cannot indemnify; keep that fallback prominent.
- Subrogation is optional and not needed for this deal.
