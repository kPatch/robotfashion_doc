# LegalZoom — Memorandum of Understanding (template wording)

Source: https://www.legalzoom.com/templates/t/memorandum-of-understanding

Lightweight, **expressly non-binding** instrument. Sections: Recitals (WHEREAS working together for [purpose]) · 1 Purpose · 2 Objectives · 3 Term (start→end unless terminated) · 4 Confidentiality (all info confidential; no third-party disclosure) · 5 Arbitration · 6 Severability · 7 Governing Law · 8 Notices · 9 Force Majeure · signatures.

How-to emphasizes: MOU is "a friendly handshake before a formal agreement," generally **not legally enforceable** unless the parties clearly intend to be bound (clear intent + mutual agreement + consideration + execution + intent to comply). Common for university-to-university collaboration.

**Verdict vs our draft:** Not a substitute for our binding funding agreement. Its real use for this project is as the **separate instrument for the Kent State / academic-collaborator relationship** (soft, advisory, non-payment) that our draft §Optional-Academic-Collaborator gestures at. Recommendation: keep the funding agreement binding; if Kent State's role needs papering, do it in a short standalone MOU rather than diluting the binding agreement. If any binding effect is desired from an MOU, state intent-to-be-bound explicitly.
